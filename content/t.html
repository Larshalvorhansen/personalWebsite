Lambda: The simulation project

A new kind of computational sandbox — where complex systems unfold in real time, and every variable tells a story.

Dr. Elena Voss March 12, 2025 6 min read

Lambda isn’t just another simulation tool. It’s a framework for building interactive worlds — from molecular dynamics to macro-economic models — with a focus on clarity and speed.

The project began as a research side project at the Institute for Computational Science, and has since grown into an open-source ecosystem used by educators, researchers, and curious minds worldwide.
