How to interpret the list

  • Flight-oriented COTS: marketed specifically for CubeSat/smallsat use. This still does not remove mission-level acceptance testing.
  • Engineering/lab COTS: ordinary commercial hardware useful for breadboards, payload development, or as a candidate part. It is not presented here as inherently space-qualified.
  • Quote required: vendor currently lists the product but does not expose a public price.
  • Active listing / request info: product is currently listed, but that is not the same as shelf stock. Use the linked vendor page to confirm the actual order date and export constraints.
  • Structures, screws, hinges and “servos”: spacecraft deployment is usually bought as a release/deployment mechanism rather than a hobby-style servo. Hinges and springs are often integrated into a deployable panel/antenna design or custom-qualified.
  • Propulsion: tank, valves, feed system, controller and propellant are commonly procured as an integrated propulsion subsystem rather than mixed-and-matched independently.
  • Biological payloads: true flight-ready biological COTS is sparse. The listed lab COTS are useful building blocks, not drop-in flight payloads.
